About us

Quality is our benchmark

Yaitu Savana Pro UG Ltd is a Ugandan construction, architectural, surveying and landscaping company working out of Fort Portal.

Who we are

Builders who start with numbers

Most disappointment on a building site comes from two things: a plan nobody costed, and a boundary nobody checked. We built this company around fixing both, which is why surveying and material estimating sit at the centre of what we do.

We work for families building a first home, traders putting up shops, schools and churches adding blocks, and land buyers who want to know what they are standing on before they pay.

  • ✓Ugandan limited company, trading as Yaitu Savana Pro UG Ltd.
  • ✓One accountable team across drawing, surveying, estimating, building and landscaping.
  • ✓Local knowledge of plots, suppliers and site conditions around Fort Portal.
