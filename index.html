What we do

One team for the whole job

Most projects go wrong between the plan and the purchase list. We keep drawing, surveying, estimating and building together, so you know what you are spending before the first brick is laid.

House plans & architectural works

Plans, elevations and layouts drawn for your plot, your budget and the way you will use the building.

Building construction

Houses, rentals, shops, schools and institutional blocks, built by a supervised team from foundation to finishing.

Surveying works

Boundary opening, plot measurement, beacons and setting-out, so what you buy is what you build on.

Landscaping works

Compound layout, paving, lawns, drainage, gardens and perimeter walls that finish a project properly.

Material estimates (BOQs)

Itemised quantities from your drawings: cement, sand, steel, roofing, before you start buying.

Planning and related works

Site assessment, project planning, supervision and other works that keep a build on budget and on time.

How we work

A straight process, no surprises

You always know what happens next, what it costs and who is on site.

  • ✓We visit the site first. We look at the plot, the soil, the access and the water before quoting anything.
  • ✓You get the numbers in writing. A bill of quantities and a clear scope, so you can compare like for like.
  • ✓One person answers for the work. A supervisor on site, with progress you can see each week.
  • ✓We hand over clean. Snag list closed, surfaces finished, compound tidy.
From first call to handover

Four steps

Site visit and brief

We meet on your plot, listen to what you want and check what the ground allows.

Survey and drawings

Boundaries, levels and plans agreed before money is committed to materials.

BOQ and programme

An itemised material estimate and a build programme you can plan around.

Build and handover

Supervised construction, weekly updates, finishing and landscaping, then a clean handover.
